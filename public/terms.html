Using mix n match

  • You may only connect music accounts that belong to you.
  • mix n match moves lists of song names between apps. It does not download, copy or share music.
  • Each music app has its own rules. mix n match works inside those rules, and an app can change or limit its service at any time.

Syncs

  • A sync checks the original playlist once a day and adds new songs to the copy. It never removes songs.
  • A sync can stop if a music app asks you to reconnect, or a playlist is removed. Your dashboard shows when that happens.

Premium

  • Premium is optional. The free plan stays free.
  • Premium renews each month or year until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from your dashboard ("Manage or cancel"); Premium then lasts until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Prices are shown on the payment page before you pay. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  • Refunds: to be decided by the owner before launch.

No perfect promises

  • Song catalogs differ between apps and countries, so some songs may not be found or may match a different version. We show you every result so you can check.
  • We are not responsible for changes made by the music apps, songs that are missing from a catalog, or actions an app takes on your account.
  • The service is provided "as is", without warranties.

Ending

You can stop using mix n match and delete your account at any time from your dashboard.
