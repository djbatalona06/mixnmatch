Privacy

Draft — have a lawyer review this before launch. Last updated: October 2026.

What we keep

  • Your email address, so you can sign in.
  • Access keys for the music apps you connect. These let us read and create playlists for you. They are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are saved, and the encryption key is stored separately.
  • Transfer results: song titles, artists and match results, so you can review them. The song list of each transfer is deleted after 30 days.
  • A security log of sign-ins, connections and transfers. The IP address in it is removed after 30 days.
  • Syncs you set up: which playlist is kept copied where, and a short fingerprint of each song already copied (so it is never copied twice). Deleted when you delete the sync.
  • If you buy Premium: your Stripe customer number and the date your paid period ends. Never your card details — Stripe handles those.

What we never do

  • We never see or store your music-app passwords.
  • We never copy, store or share any audio. We only handle song names and IDs.
  • We never sell your data or use it for ads.
  • We only ask the music apps for the permissions needed to read and create playlists and liked songs.

Your controls

  • Disconnect any app on your dashboard: its access key is deleted at once. You can also remove mix n match from the app's own settings.
  • Delete your account on your dashboard: your email, keys, syncs and transfer history are removed, and any Premium plan is cancelled.

Who helps us run mix n match

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and database.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails.
  • Stripe handles Premium payments (only if you buy Premium).
  • The music apps you connect (Spotify, TIDAL, Deezer, Google/YouTube, SoundCloud) receive requests made on your behalf.
